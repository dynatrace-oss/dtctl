## Prompt

Did any Node.js process run out of heap memory ("JavaScript heap out of
memory") in the last 7 days? If so, when, and in which pod and cluster?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `occurrences` (timestamp, status, cluster, namespace, pod of
every matching log line in 7 days) and `count`; measured at batch start and
end.

- 3: all occurrences (dates within an hour) with the pod/workload and
  cluster.
- 2: at least one occurrence with location, or all dates without location.
- 1: no occurrence found, but the answer flags that the search was partial or
  limited to a shorter window.
- 0: "none" without caveat, or no answer.
