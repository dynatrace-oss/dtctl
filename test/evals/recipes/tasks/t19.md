## Prompt

Users say the `${EVAL_T1_AGENT_ENDPOINT}` endpoint of the
`${EVAL_T1_AGENT_SERVICE}` service (production) is slow. Investigate: how long
does a typical request take, and where is the time spent?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `p50_s`/`p90_s` of the endpoint's root spans (2h), and
`breakdown` — the share of trace time in LLM chat calls vs tool executions vs
other work.

- 3: typical duration in the right range (p50 within ±40%) AND identifies that
  the time is dominated by sequential LLM (chat/model) calls, with tool
  executions negligible — evidenced from spans.
- 2: right duration and a partially right breakdown (e.g. names LLM calls but
  also blames tools, or no quantification).
- 1: right duration only, or right breakdown with wrong duration.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
