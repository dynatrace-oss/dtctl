## Prompt

Around ${EVAL_T2_CHANGE_TIME}, the workload `${EVAL_T2_CHANGE_WORKLOAD}` in
namespace `${EVAL_T2_CHANGE_NAMESPACE}` started misbehaving. What changed
shortly before that, and how did it show up afterwards?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `events` / `about_workload` lists the Davis events in the
namespace from 3 hours before to 30 minutes after the given time, with the
first/last time and a description.

- 3: names the specific change from the earliest change-type event about the
  workload (what field changed, from which value to which, roughly when) AND
  the symptom that followed (from the later events, e.g. throttling).
- 2: finds the change event and its time but not what changed, OR names what
  changed without connecting it to the symptom.
- 1: only generic findings (e.g. "a deployment happened", a version event, or
  only the symptom) without the actual change.
- 0: "nothing changed", wrong workload/time, or no answer.
