## Prompt

Which workflow ran most often over the last 7 days, how many executions did
it have, and did any workflow fail every time it ran in that period?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_workflow` (final executions in 7d per workflow title, with
failures), and `always_failed` (workflows whose every execution failed),
start and end of batch.

- 3: the most frequent workflow with its count within ±20%, AND the
  workflow(s) in `always_failed` (or that there are none).
- 2: one of the two parts right.
- 1: the right workflow from an incomplete listing, with a count that is
  clearly one page of executions.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
