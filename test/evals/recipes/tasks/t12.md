## Prompt

How many log records with status ERROR were ingested between 24 hours ago and
12 hours ago?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `count` for the window [now-24h, now-12h), measured at batch
start and end (the window moves with the clock).

- 3: within ±20% of either measurement.
- 2: within ±50%.
- 1: right order of magnitude.
- 0: 0, wildly off, or no answer.
