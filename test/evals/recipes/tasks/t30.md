## Prompt

Which error log messages showed up for the first time on ${EVAL_T1_NEW_DAY}
(UTC), compared with the six days before? Give the top ones with how often
they occurred that day.

## Rubric

Ground truth: `new_templates` (error templates seen on the day and never in
the six days before, with counts and first occurrence) and
`top_templates_of_day_that_are_not_new` (the day's frequent errors that also
occurred before), measured at batch start and end.

- 3: names the top new template(s) of `new_templates` with a count within
  ±35%, and does not present old templates as new.
- 2: names the top new template but count missing/off, or mixes one old
  template into the list.
- 1: lists the day's most frequent errors without a baseline comparison
  (mostly templates from `top_templates_of_day_that_are_not_new`).
- 0: no answer, or "nothing new" when `new_templates` is not empty.
