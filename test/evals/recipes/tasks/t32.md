## Prompt

How many LLM input tokens were consumed in this environment over the last 24
hours, and which service consumed the most?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `input_tokens`/`calls` and `by_service` after de-duplicating
calls recorded twice; `naive_input_tokens` and `naive_by_service` are the
inflated sums, measured at batch start and end.

- 3: total within ±20% of `input_tokens` AND the top service of `by_service`.
- 2: one of the two right; or naive numbers reported with an explicit,
  quantified double-counting caveat.
- 1: the naive total (±20% of `naive_input_tokens`) and/or the naive top
  service, presented as the answer.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
