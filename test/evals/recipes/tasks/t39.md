## Prompt

Over the last 24 hours, how often did users see product tiles and how often
did they click one, per application? What is the click-through rate?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_provider_and_type` (24h counts of tile impression and click
business events per provider/application); measured at batch start and end.

- 3: impressions and clicks per application within ±20%, with a
  click-through rate consistent with them.
- 2: the main application's numbers right, the other missing or wrong; or
  totals right without the split.
- 1: right event types with counts from a shorter (default) window.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
