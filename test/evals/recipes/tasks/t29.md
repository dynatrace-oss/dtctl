## Prompt

What errors has the `${EVAL_T2_LOG_WORKLOAD}` workload in namespace
`${EVAL_T2_LOG_NAMESPACE}` (cluster `${EVAL_T2_LOG_CLUSTER}`) logged in the last
hour? Give the most frequent error messages with counts.

## Rubric

Ground truth: `totals` (records and ERROR records from the workload's pods in
the last hour; `via_workload_field` = what a k8s.workload.name filter finds)
and `top_errors` (count and example per error template), measured at batch
start and end.

- 3: the top error message(s) of `top_errors` AND an ERROR count within ±30%.
- 2: right top message, count missing or off by more than 30%.
- 1: says the workload logged no errors / no logs found, but flags that the
  logs may not be attributed to the workload (or reports a related signal).
- 0: "no errors" without caveat, a different workload's errors, or no answer.
