## Prompt

How many distinct open, not-muted vulnerabilities rated high or critical
(Dynatrace Security Score ≥ 7) are there right now, and how many of those are
critical (score ≥ 9)?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `high_or_critical` and `critical` (latest state per
vulnerability and affected entity, open and not muted on at least one entity),
measured at batch start and end. `capped` = 50 is what a capped listing shows.

- 3: both numbers within ±15% of GT.
- 2: one of the two within ±15%, the other wrong; or both within ±35%.
- 1: reports the 50-row cap as the total, or counts raw events (order of
  magnitude too high), with a correct critical count.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
