## Prompt

How many log records did the service `${EVAL_T1_LOG_SERVICE}` write in the
last hour, across all of its environments?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `count` = records with service.name equal to the service in the
last hour; `workload_only` = records found via k8s.workload.name only (the
partial-carriage undercount); start and end of batch.

- 3: within ±20% of `count`.
- 2: within ±40%, or the right number for one environment clearly labelled.
- 1: ≈ `workload_only` (undercount) or another partial route.
- 0: 0 / no answer / wildly off.
