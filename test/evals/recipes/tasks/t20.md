## Prompt

Over the last 7 days, which Davis problem category occurred most often, how
many problems was that, and what was their median time to resolution?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `top_category`, `count`, `median_min` (closed, non-duplicate
problems with an update in 7d).

- 3: category AND count within ±10% AND median within ±30%.
- 2: category and count right, median wrong/missing (or mean given as median
  and labelled so).
- 1: category right only.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
