## Prompt

Over the last 7 days, how many Davis problems affected the Kubernetes
namespace `${EVAL_T2_NAMESPACE}`? Ignore problems Davis marked as duplicates.
What was the most frequent problem title among them?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `count` (non-duplicate problems with an update in the last 7
days whose k8s.namespace.name array contains the namespace), `top_title` and
`top_title_count`. `count_with_duplicates` is what you get when the
duplicate filter is forgotten; `eq_count` is what the `==` trap returns (0).

- 3: count within ±15% of `count` AND top title correct.
- 2: count within ±15% but title wrong/missing, OR title correct and count
  within ±35% (e.g. a slightly different window or counting duplicates).
- 1: a non-zero but badly wrong count.
- 0: reports 0 / "no problems" (the `==` trap), or no answer.
