## Prompt

Across all events in the events table over the last 24 hours, which event
kind was the most frequent, and how many events of that kind were there?
Which kind comes second?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_kind` lists event kinds with 24h counts (start and end of
batch).

- 3: the top kind with its count within ±20%, AND the right second kind.
- 2: the top kind with its count off by 20–50%, or the right count without
  the second kind or with the wrong second kind.
- 1: the right top kind with a count that is clearly the 2h default window,
  or only Kubernetes events counted.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
