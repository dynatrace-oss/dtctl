## Prompt

Is `${EVAL_T2_EXC_SERVICE}` handling its requests cleanly in the last hour, or
is something going wrong that its failure rate does not show?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `requests` (root requests, failed roots, roots carrying an
exception) and `exceptions` (exception type and message by span kind, with how
many of those spans are failed requests), measured at batch start and end.

- 3: names the dominant exception type AND its message (what it is about)
  with a count or rate in the right range (±40%) AND says those requests are
  not counted as failed (failure rate stays near zero).
- 2: right exception and message, count missing/off or the failed-vs-not
  point missing.
- 1: reports failure rate/latency only and calls the service healthy with a
  caveat, or mentions "exceptions" without type or message.
- 0: "healthy, nothing wrong", or no answer.
