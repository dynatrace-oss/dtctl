## Prompt

Something seems off in the Kubernetes cluster `${EVAL_T2_CLUSTER}`. Investigate
which workloads are unhealthy right now (crash-looping, failing, not ready) and
explain the most likely cause for the worst one.

## Rubric

Ground truth: `unhealthy` lists the workloads with Warning events in the last
hour (workload, namespace, reasons, counts); `cause` describes what the worst
workload's own logs show.

- 3: names the worst workload from `unhealthy` with its failure mode
  (e.g. BackOff / BackoffLimitExceeded) AND a cause consistent with `cause`
  (evidence from its logs/messages).
- 2: names the right workload and failure mode, but the cause is missing,
  generic ("check logs") or not evidenced.
- 1: points at the right namespace or a secondary symptom, or lists the
  workload among many without identifying it as the worst.
- 0: "everything is healthy", wrong workload, or no answer.
