## Prompt

Among services that handled at least 1,000 requests in the last 2 hours,
which one has the highest failure rate? Give its failure rate and the dominant
reason its requests fail (for example an HTTP status code or an exception).

## Rubric

Ground truth: `top` lists the services with ≥1,000 requests ranked by failure
rate (name, requests, failures, rate %); `reason` is the dominant failure
reason of the top service from its failed root spans.

- 3: a service whose rate is within 10% (relative) of the top one (near-ties
  count) AND rate within ±30% relative of that service's GT rate AND the
  failure reason consistent with `reason`.
- 2: right service and rate, reason missing or wrong; OR right service and
  reason with a rate off by more than 30%.
- 1: names a high-failure service that is not near the top, or right service
  with no usable numbers.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
