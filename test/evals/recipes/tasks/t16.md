## Prompt

How much log data, in GiB, was billed for "Log Management & Analytics – Ingest
& Process" over the last 7 complete UTC days (not counting today)?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `ingest_gib` (billed bytes / 2^30 over the 7 whole UTC days
before today, attributed by usage start, events de-duplicated).

- 3: within ±10% of `ingest_gib`.
- 2: within ±25% (e.g. a rolling 7×24h window instead of whole days, or GB vs GiB).
- 1: right order of magnitude, or the retain number instead of ingest.
- 0: wrong unit by ≥1000× / no answer.
