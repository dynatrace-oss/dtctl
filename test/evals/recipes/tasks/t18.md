## Prompt

Over the last 24 hours, how many LLM calls did the applications in this
environment make, how many input tokens did they consume in total, and what
share of the input tokens was served from the prompt cache?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `calls`, `input_tokens`, `cache_read_tokens`, `cache_share_pct`
over 24h (start and end of batch).

- 3: calls within ±20%, input tokens within ±20%, cache share within ±5 points.
- 2: two of the three right.
- 1: one of the three right (e.g. a 2h window extrapolated badly).
- 0: none right / no answer.
