## Prompt

Which SLOs in this environment are currently not meeting their target, and
by how much?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `slos` (name, target, warning, evaluated value and status per
SLO) and `not_meeting`; measured at batch start and end.

- 3: exactly the SLOs in `not_meeting`, each with value and target (±2
  points), and says the others are fine.
- 2: the right SLO(s) without values, or with one extra/missing SLO.
- 1: lists the SLOs and targets without evaluating them.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
