## Prompt

How many log records were stored in this environment over the last 7 days in
total?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `total_7d_estimate` (sampled count scaled by the sampling
ratio), validated against an exact 24h count (`exact_24h` vs `estimate_24h`).

- 3: a total within ±20% of `total_7d_estimate`, with the method stated
  (e.g. per-day sums, an unlimited scan, or a sampling-based estimate).
- 2: within ±40%, or a correct daily volume with a loose extrapolation.
- 1: a partial count, but explicitly flagged as incomplete / a lower bound.
- 0: a partial count presented as the total, or no answer.
