## Prompt

Which log source wrote the most log records in the last hour, and how many
records was that?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_source` (log records per log.source in the last hour),
start and end of batch.

- 3: the top named source AND its count within ±25%.
- 2: the right source with a count off by more than 25%.
- 1: records without a source counted as the top source, or a ranking by
  another field (host, service) instead of log.source.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
