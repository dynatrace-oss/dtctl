## Prompt

How many open vulnerabilities does this environment have right now, and how
many distinct entities are affected by them?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `vulnerabilities` and `entities` (latest state per
vulnerability and affected entity, open and not muted), `pairs` = the number
of (vulnerability, entity) combinations; measured at batch start and end.

- 3: both numbers within ±15%.
- 2: one within ±15%, the other wrong or missing; or both within ±35%; or
  `pairs` reported and clearly labelled as such.
- 1: a row cap (e.g. 50/100/1000) or raw event counts reported as the total.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
