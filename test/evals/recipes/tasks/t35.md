## Prompt

Have any containers been killed for running out of memory in the last 7 days?
If so, which workloads, how many times, and roughly when?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_workload` (OOM kills per workload over 7 days, with daily
buckets oldest to newest), `total`, and `last_2h_total` (what a default
window shows); measured at batch start and end.

- 3: names the top workload(s) of `by_workload` with counts within ±30% and
  roughly when (which days).
- 2: names the top workload with a count off by more than 30%, or misses
  most of the others.
- 1: reports OOM signals (events/restarts) without workloads or counts, or
  answers for a shorter window with a caveat.
- 0: "no OOM kills" when `total` > 0, or no answer.
