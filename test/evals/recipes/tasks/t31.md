## Prompt

Between ${EVAL_T2_SHIFT_FROM} and ${EVAL_T2_SHIFT_TO} UTC, which endpoints of
`${EVAL_T2_SHIFT_SERVICE}` got slower compared with the hour before, and by how
much?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `shifted` (endpoints ranked by p90 in the window vs the hour
before, with p50/p90 for both windows) and `slowest_in_focus_window` (the
absolute ranking, which a wrong approach would report).

- 3: identifies the group of endpoints that shifted most in `shifted` (they
  share a pattern) with before/after values in the right range (±40%).
- 2: right endpoints, magnitudes missing or off; or one endpoint right with
  correct numbers and the rest missing.
- 1: reports the absolute slowest endpoints (including one that is slow in
  both windows) without a comparison, even if it partly overlaps.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
