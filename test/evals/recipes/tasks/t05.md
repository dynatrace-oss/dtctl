## Prompt

Which host's disk is closest to being full? Tell me the host, the mount point
(or drive) and how much free space is left on it.

## Rubric

Ground truth: `top` lists the fullest filesystems (host, mount, used %, free GiB).

- 3: the top host AND mount AND free space within ±50% (or "~0.1 GiB" /
  "about 100 MB" for a near-empty disk).
- 2: right host and mount, free space missing or in a wrong unit.
- 1: right host, wrong/missing mount.
- 0: wrong host / no answer.
