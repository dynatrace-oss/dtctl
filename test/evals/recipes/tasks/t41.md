## Prompt

Over the last 7 days, which provider sent the most business events, and what
share of all business events in that period came from it?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_provider` (7d counts per provider) and `total_7d`, start
and end of batch.

- 3: the top provider AND its share within ±5 percentage points.
- 2: the top provider with a share off by more than 5 points, or with
  counts but no share.
- 1: the top provider from a window clearly shorter than 7 days, or the
  runner-up provider.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
