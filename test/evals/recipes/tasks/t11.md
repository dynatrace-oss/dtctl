## Prompt

Which synthetic monitor failed most often in the last 24 hours? Give the
number of failed executions and the reason they fail.

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_monitor` lists monitors with failed/total executions in 24h
and the failure status code/message.

- 3: top monitor AND failed count within ±20% AND the failure reason
  (status code or message) correct.
- 2: top monitor + count, reason missing/wrong; or monitor + reason with count off.
- 1: a failing monitor that is not the top one.
- 0: wrong / no answer / "no failures".
