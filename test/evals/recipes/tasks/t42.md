## Prompt

Which HTTP synthetic monitor had the slowest average execution time over the
last 24 hours, and what was that average?

## Rubric

Ground truth: `by_monitor` (HTTP monitor executions in 24h: count and mean
duration in ms), start and end of batch.

- 3: the slowest monitor AND its average within ±20% (in a stated unit).
- 2: the right monitor with the average off by more than 20%, or the unit
  wrong by a factor of 1000 or more.
- 1: a ranking over step executions or browser monitors instead of HTTP
  monitor executions, naming another monitor.
- 0: wrong / no answer.
